Sketch Scans

Browse the cave's scanned sketches, and put one under the drawing at the right size and angle. ss


The cave's scans as a tree, a preview beside it, and the tabs for Plan, Profile and Cross Section.

When you reach for it

Before any tracing. A scan that is not lined up with the survey is worse than no scan: every wall you trace off it is wrong by the same amount.

Steps

  1. Pick the tab for the job — Plan, Profile or Cross Section. Each holds only the buttons that view uses, in the order they are used.
  2. Align on Scan picks the stations on the scan itself in the zoomable viewer — wheel to zoom, middle-drag to pan, left-click a station and name it from the drawing's own list — then places the scan already fitted. No insert-then-hunt-then-align.
  3. Insert & Align instead drops the scan over the survey at a guessed size and fits it there: click a station on the image, click where it belongs. Two stations for a plain fit; three or more also take out the scanner's own stretch and skew, with a report of how far the worst station missed by.
  4. Add a scan from elsewhere... file-picks a page the panel's list cannot reach — a photo still on a phone, a page scanned to the Desktop — into the same path.
  5. Right-click a scan for Mark Complete. A tick appears beside it, and on a trip folder when every page in it is done.

You can also drag image files straight onto the preview. They are copied into the cave's scans folder — into whichever trip folder is selected — never moved, and never over the top of a file already there.

Try it on Truitt

Put a scan under the drawing twice without touching the menu bar: once from the cave's own scans folder, once with Add a scan from elsewhere....

When it refuses

  • A scan whose file has moved is reported rather than silently blank — put it back with the relink path.
