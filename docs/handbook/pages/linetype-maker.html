Linetype Maker

Make, edit and import your own linetypes. ltm

When you reach for it

When a line on your map needs to say what it is along its whole length: a stream that repeats W, a boundary that repeats LIMIT, or a dash pattern the template does not have.

How it works

  1. Pick a linetype from the list, or press New.
  2. Give it a name: letters, digits, _ - $, no spaces.
  3. Edit the rows. A positive Length draws a dash, a negative one leaves a gap, 0 is a dot. Type in Text and it is drawn at the end of that row; Size is its height, X and Y nudge it, Rot° turns it.
  4. Watch the preview. Anything wrong is listed under it.
  5. Save to Library to keep it; Apply to Drawing to use it in the open drawing.

Bringing your own

Import… reads an AutoCAD .lin file, or the linetypes inside another drawing. Tick the ones you want; a name you already have says so, and ticking it replaces yours.

Good to know

Your library is one file beside your caves, Documents/Cave/linetypes/CaveCustomLinetypes.lin. It syncs with them and no CaveCAD update ever replaces it. Every new cave map gets the whole library.

Text inside a linetype cannot contain spaces or commas.

Text linetypes are saved as real DXF linetypes, so other CAD programs draw them too.

Symbols drawn into a line (ticks, chevrons) are coming next; until then use Shaped Lines.
