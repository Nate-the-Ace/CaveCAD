Generate Profile

Rebuild the extended elevation beside the plan: one band per survey run, floor and ceiling lines from LRUD and splays. gp

When you reach for it

Rarely. It happens on its own with every draw, from the notebook's own survey model. This forces it from the drawing's own tags instead, and prints what it could not show.

Steps

  1. Cave Survey > Generate Profile.
  2. Read the report: a run it could not band is named rather than skipped silently.

Pitches

Every drop the band draws is labelled beside the middle of its line — P 187 ft (62 + 125). In the plan the number exists because a pitch has no size at all; here it exists for the opposite reason. The drop is the most prominent thing on the page and the one line with no scale cue of its own: no walls either side, no bends, nothing but two station points at its ends.

The text is the same text the plan draws, from the same place, so the two views cannot disagree about how deep a drop is.

A drop the band does not draw whole gets no number. A run with two arms keeps the longer one, so a pitch down the shorter arm has its bottom nowhere on the page — and half a line with a depth beside it would be a claim about geometry the reader cannot see. The plan still labels it: the plan never splits a cave into bands.
