Put a sheet together at a scale that fits

The map becomes paper: a border, a scale bar, a north arrow, a title block.

Steps

  1. Run Check Map first and work its list down. A sheet built over a fault prints the fault.
  2. Cave Survey > Sheet Setup.
  3. Pick the paper. It proposes the most detailed standard plot scale the plan fits at — take it unless you have a reason.
  4. Tick the furniture. The preview redraws as you tick: the paper, the cave's footprint, the furniture, and where the elevation bands land.
  5. Read the warnings. It says plainly when something is off the paper and which thing — the question you would otherwise answer by building the file and looking.
  6. Build Sheet. One sheet per file: <Cave> Plan Sheet.dxf, plus <Cave> Profile Sheet.dxf when the elevation is ticked.

Metric

The ratios at the bottom of the list — 1:100 to 1:2000 — are unit-free, and a metric sheet gets a bar counted in metres captioned with its ratio. A bar marked in feet under "1:500" asks the reader to convert in their head.

What never reaches a sheet

No raster at all: not your scanned sketches, not the aerial photograph. A scan is a tracing reference, and everything worth keeping off it has been traced already.
