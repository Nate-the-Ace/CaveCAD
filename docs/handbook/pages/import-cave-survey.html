Import Cave Survey

Read a survey file into the drawing. Compass .dat, Walls .srv, Survex .svx, Therion .th or CSV. ics

When you reach for it

When the shots already exist somewhere else — an old project, a teammate's file, or a phone. Therion is what TopoDroid and PocketTopo export, so a phone survey comes in through the same door.

Steps

  1. Cave Survey > Import Cave Survey and pick the file. The format is detected for you.
  2. If Therion sketch pages (.th2) sit beside the file, you are asked whether to bring the drawing in too. See below.
  3. Check the trips that arrive in Survey Notebook — dates, teams, declination.

The sketch, not just the numbers

A Therion project is two halves: the .th with the shots, and one .th2 page per sketched scrap. TopoDroid writes both. Importing only the numbers left you re-tracing, by hand, a photograph of a drawing you already had in vector — so if .th2 files sit beside the survey file, they are offered in the same gesture.

What arrives is real map ink, not a tracing reference: walls land on WALLS-SURVEYED, a presumed wall on WALLS-INFERRED, ledges and pits arrive as shaped lines with their ornament, areas as real areas that regenerate, and symbols as catalogue blocks aimed and sized the way the sketch asked. You can extend, edit and plot every piece of it exactly as if you had traced it yourself.

Each page is fitted to its station markers and then bent onto them, which is what Therion does and what makes the sketch follow a survey that has since been adjusted. The report quotes how far the worst station was out before the bending — that is the honest measure of whether the sketch and the survey agree, and a large number is a page worth going to look at.

What is not imported is said out loud, every time and by name: a page drawn in a projection this suite does not carry, a page tied to stations this drawing has never heard of, and any Therion type not in the mapping (those are drawn where the sketch put them, on NOTES-ANNOTATION, so nothing is lost).

Import the same sketch again and each page it already holds is asked about, not overwritten: by then you have probably worked on the ink it put here. A backup is taken before anything is replaced.
