One panel for putting marks on the map: Trace, Symbols and Areas. draw


Trace and Symbols sharing row one, Areas across row two.

When you reach for it

Whenever you are drawing. Cave drawing is one job done with one hand — a wall, then the ledge below it, then the gravel filling the floor between them — so the three used to be separate docks and you were choosing which panel to look at before choosing what to draw.

Steps

  1. Cave Survey > Draw, or ft, sym or area — all three open this panel with that section unfolded.
  2. Sections sit two to a row: Trace and Symbols share row one, Areas spans row two.
  3. Fold a section by its header; it keeps its cell rather than reflowing the grid.
  4. Right-click a header for Move Up, Move Down, Reset Order. What you fold and how you order is remembered.
