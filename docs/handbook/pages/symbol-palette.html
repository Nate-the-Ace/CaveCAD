Symbol Palette

Every cave symbol the template carries, grouped by category, each tile showing the symbol itself rather than its name. symbolpalette

When you reach for it

For everything that is a mark rather than a line: entrances, pits, domes, formations, flow arrows.

Steps

  1. Pick a symbol and click in the drawing to place it.
  2. Or press and drag instead: the symbol turns to face the cursor and takes its size from how far you dragged — the distance from the press point is its radius. Aimed and sized in one gesture.
  3. The next plain click repeats what the last drag asked for, so a row of six identical flow arrows is one drag and five clicks.
  4. Where you click decides the view, exactly as it does for Feature Trace. The readout above the tiles names the layer under the cursor before you press.
  5. Return with one symbol left showing arms it. The Recent strip holds the last five.

There are no size or angle boxes to type into: the drag sets both.

Your own symbols

New Symbol... opens a drawing to draw one in, centred on its origin with a 10 ft working square — the biggest a symbol is meant to be. Give it a name, a category and a home layer, and it is written into your own library at ~/Documents/Cave/symbols/CaveCustomSymbols.dxf, beside your caves, where it syncs and gets backed up with them and no CaveCAD update can reach it.

When it refuses

  • The symbols the suite ships cannot be edited or deleted — the next update would take the change back. Use Duplicate as New Symbol... and edit the copy.
  • A drawing that does not have a symbol — an old cave, or one never started from the template — gets the block fetched from the template as the symbol is placed.
